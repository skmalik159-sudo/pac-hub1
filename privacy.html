Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

Kamra Community Hub is an independent community platform for residents, families, and local businesses of Kamra and surrounding areas. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government entity or organization.

Information We Collect

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo
  • Phone, WhatsApp, or other contact details you choose to provide
  • City, area, or location text you enter
  • Community posts, listings, and announcements
  • Images uploaded by users
  • Private messages sent through the app
  • Blood group and availability, if you use the blood donor features
  • Reports, blocks, and moderation records
  • Device information and push notification token for app functionality

Authentication

Users sign in using email and password.

Private Messages

Users may send and receive messages inside the app. Message content, sender and receiver account identifiers, timestamps, and related conversation details may be stored.

Messages are stored to provide the communication features of the app, and to support safety, moderation, abuse prevention, and user support.

Phone, WhatsApp, and Contact Details

Users may choose to provide a phone number, WhatsApp number, or other contact details in a profile, post, listing, blood request, or message.

Where you choose to share these details, they may be visible to other users of the app. Please only share contact details that you are comfortable making available to others.

Blood Donor and Blood Request Information

Users may voluntarily submit a blood group, city or area, availability, and contact information in order to use the blood donor and blood request features.

This information can be sensitive, and it may be visible to other users so that donors and requesters can be connected.

Kamra Community Hub is not a medical provider. The app does not verify blood groups, medical suitability, donor eligibility, or the accuracy of any emergency information. For urgent medical needs, please contact qualified medical or emergency services directly.

Posts, Listings, and Images

Community posts, buy and sell listings, jobs, rentals, services, announcements, images, and profile details may be publicly visible to other users within the app.

Content may be reviewed, moderated, removed, or restricted if it breaks the community rules or safety requirements. Users are responsible for the content they post.

Kamra Community Hub does not verify users, donors, employers, job posts, sellers, buyers, properties, businesses, or services listed in the app.

Photos

Users may upload profile pictures and post images.

Push Notifications

The app may store a device push notification token in order to send alerts about posts, messages, account activity, moderation decisions, or community updates.

You can control or turn off notifications at any time in your device settings.

City, Area, and Location

The app may collect city, area, or location text that you enter yourself, such as Kamra, Attock, or nearby areas.

Kamra Community Hub does not collect GPS or precise location. If a future feature needs precise location, it will ask for your permission first.

Safety, Moderation, and Reports

Reports submitted by users, moderation actions, blocked users, admin review records, and related metadata may be stored in order to keep the community safe and to prevent abuse.

Storage

Data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL database and file storage), with row-level security restricting access to authorized users only.

Security

Reasonable security measures are used to protect user data.

Data Retention

Data is kept for as long as it is needed to provide the features of the app, to meet safety and legal needs, to prevent abuse, or until deletion is requested.

You can request deletion using the deletion page or the contact method below. After a deletion request, some records may still be retained for a limited period for security, backups, dispute handling, or legal compliance.
